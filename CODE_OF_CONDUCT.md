# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include
respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, and accepting responsibility
for mistakes. Harassment, personal attacks, publication of private information,
or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through the contact channel in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or otherwise
respond to contributions and comments that do not align with this Code of
Conduct.
